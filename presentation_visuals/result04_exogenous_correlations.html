Insight: The strongest correlation is Temperature vs Humidity at r = -0.42 (moderate negative). This aligns with atmospheric physics: as temperature rises, relative humidity drops because warmer air has a higher saturation vapour pressure (Clausius-Clapeyron relation). Precipitation shows a weak negative correlation (-0.23), meaning hotter days tend to be drier on average. Wind and pressure have weak positive relationships. These correlations validate the choice of exogenous regressors in the SARIMAX model — humidity in particular carries meaningful predictive signal for temperature forecasting.
